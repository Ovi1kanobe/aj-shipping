When a medical foundation in Miami needed to urgently ship life-saving supplies to hospitals in Havana, Crev Shipping stepped up to ensure rapid delivery. Despite challenging logistics and tight customs requirements, we successfully delivered the complete shipment within 48 hours.

Our team coordinated closely with both US and Cuban customs authorities to expedite the clearance process. The shipment included essential medical equipment, pharmaceuticals, and surgical supplies that were desperately needed at three major hospitals in Havana.
