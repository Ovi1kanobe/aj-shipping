Crev Shipping specializes in reliable shipping services to Haiti, connecting the Haitian diaspora with their families and communities. We understand the importance of these connections and have built a robust network to serve all areas of Haiti, from bustling urban centers to remote mountain villages.

Our deep knowledge of Haitian logistics and customs procedures ensures your packages reach their destination efficiently, whether sending essential goods, personal items, or business supplies.

### Major Cities & Departments

We provide comprehensive coverage to all Haitian departments and major cities:

1. **Port-au-Prince** - Capital city and Ouest Department
2. **Cap-Haïtien** - Major city in Nord Department  
3. **Gonaïves** - Artibonite Department regional center
4. **Les Cayes** - Sud Department coastal city
5. **Fort-Liberté** - Nord-Est Department
6. **Hinche** - Centre Department
7. **Jérémie** - Grand'Anse Department
8. **Jacmel** - Sud-Est Department historic port city
9. **Port-de-Paix** - Nord-Ouest Department
10. **Miragoâne** - Nippes Department

### Rural & Remote Areas

Our extensive network reaches even the most remote areas of Haiti:

- **Central Plateau** - Rural farming communities in Centre Department
- **Northern Mountains** - Remote villages in Massif du Nord
- **Southern Peninsula** - Isolated communities in Grand'Anse and Sud
- **Artibonite Valley** - Rural agricultural regions
- **Mountain Villages** - High-altitude communities throughout the country
- **Coastal Fishing Towns** - Remote seaside communities
- **Border Regions** - Communities along the Dominican border

### Access Methods

We utilize multiple transportation methods to ensure nationwide coverage:

1. **Port-au-Prince Hub** - Main distribution center via Toussaint Louverture International Airport
2. **Cap-Haïtien Network** - Northern distribution via Hugo Chávez International Airport
3. **Ground Transport** - Extensive road network for rural delivery
4. **Coastal Shipping** - Service to remote coastal areas
5. **Mountain Access** - Specialized transport for mountainous regions

- Essential household items and clothing
- Non-perishable food items and supplies
- Electronics and appliances
- Educational materials and school supplies
- Medical supplies and medications (with documentation)
- Construction materials and tools
- Personal care products
