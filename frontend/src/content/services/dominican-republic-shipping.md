Crev Shipping offers comprehensive shipping services throughout Brazil, the largest country in South America. Our extensive logistics network covers everything from major metropolitan areas to remote Amazon communities, ensuring your packages reach their destination regardless of location.

We understand Brazil's complex geography and have established partnerships with local carriers to provide reliable delivery across all 26 states and the Federal District.

### Major Cities & Metropolitan Areas

We provide excellent coverage to Brazil's largest cities and urban centers:

1. **São Paulo** - Largest metropolitan area in South America
2. **Rio de Janeiro** - Major coastal city and cultural center
3. **Brasília** - Federal capital and surrounding Federal District
4. **Salvador** - Major city in Bahia state
5. **Fortaleza** - Northeastern coastal metropolis
6. **Belo Horizonte** - Major city in Minas Gerais
7. **Manaus** - Amazon region's largest city
8. **Curitiba** - Southern Brazil's major urban center
9. **Recife** - Northeastern coastal city
10. **Porto Alegre** - Southern Brazil's major port city

### Regional Coverage

Our network extends to all Brazilian regions:

- **Southeast (Sudeste)** - São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, Minas Gerais, Espírito Santo
- **South (Sul)** - Rio Grande do Sul, Santa Catarina, Paraná
- **Northeast (Nordeste)** - All 9 northeastern states including remote inland areas
- **North (Norte)** - Amazon region including Amazonas, Pará, Acre, Roraima
- **Central-West (Centro-Oeste)** - Mato Grosso, Mato Grosso do Sul, Goiás

### Remote & Rural Areas

We specialize in reaching Brazil's most challenging destinations:

- **Amazon Rainforest** - Remote communities accessible only by river
- **Pantanal Wetlands** - Remote cattle ranching and conservation areas
- **Cerrado Savanna** - Rural agricultural communities in central Brazil
- **Caatinga Region** - Semi-arid northeastern interior communities
- **Atlantic Forest Remnants** - Isolated coastal mountain communities
- **Border Towns** - Remote communities along international borders
- **Island Communities** - Coastal and river islands throughout Brazil

### Access Methods

We utilize Brazil's diverse transportation infrastructure:

1. **Air Freight** - Major airports in São Paulo, Rio, Brasília, and regional hubs
2. **Ocean Ports** - Santos, Rio de Janeiro, Salvador, and other coastal ports
3. **Highway Network** - Extensive road system connecting all major cities
4. **River Transport** - Amazon and Pantanal waterway access
5. **Regional Airlines** - Small aircraft service to remote airstrips
6. **Local Partnerships** - Last-mile delivery through Brazilian carriers

- Industrial equipment and machinery
- Educational and professional materials
