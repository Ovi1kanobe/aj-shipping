Crev Shipping provides comprehensive shipping services to the Dominican Republic, serving the entire island of Hispaniola's eastern portion. Our established network connects major urban centers with remote mountain villages and coastal communities throughout all 31 provinces.

We understand the Dominican Republic's diverse geography and have built strong partnerships with local carriers to ensure reliable delivery from the bustling capital to the most remote rural areas.

### Major Cities & Urban Centers

We provide excellent coverage to all major Dominican cities:

1. **Santo Domingo** - Capital city and National District
2. **Santiago de los Caballeros** - Second largest city in Cibao region
3. **Santo Domingo Este** - Major eastern metropolitan area
4. **Santo Domingo Oeste** - Western metropolitan expansion
5. **San Pedro de Macorís** - Major eastern port city
6. **La Romana** - Eastern tourist and sugar region center
7. **San Francisco de Macorís** - Northeastern regional hub
8. **Puerto Plata** - Northern coast major port and tourist center
9. **San Cristóbal** - Important southern city
10. **Higüey** - Eastern religious and tourist center

### Provincial Coverage

Our network reaches all 31 provinces across the country:

- **Cibao Region** - Santiago, La Vega, Moca, and northern provinces
- **East Region** - La Romana, San Pedro de Macorís, El Seibo
- **South Region** - San Cristóbal, Azua, Barahona
- **North Coast** - Puerto Plata, Monte Cristi, Samaná
- **Central Mountains** - San Juan, Elías Piña, Dajabón
- **Southwest** - Bahoruco, Independencia, Pedernales

### Remote & Rural Areas

We specialize in reaching challenging and remote destinations:

- **Central Mountain Range (Cordillera Central)** - High-altitude communities including Pico Duarte region
- **Sierra de Neiba** - Southwestern mountain communities
- **Sierra de Bahoruco** - Southern border mountain villages
- **Remote Beach Communities** - Isolated coastal fishing villages
- **Border Towns** - Communities along the Haitian border
- **Coffee Plantation Areas** - Mountain coffee farming regions
- **Rural Sugar Cane Areas** - Remote agricultural communities
- **Island Communities** - Saona and Catalina islands

### Access Methods

We utilize the Dominican Republic's well-developed transportation network:

1. **Air Freight** - Las Américas International Airport (Santo Domingo), Cibao International (Santiago)
2. **Port Services** - Santo Domingo, Puerto Plata, and regional ports
3. **Highway System** - Excellent road network connecting all major cities
4. **Mountain Routes** - Specialized transport for mountainous regions
5. **Coastal Access** - Service to remote beach and fishing communities
6. **Border Crossings** - Efficient customs and border procedures
