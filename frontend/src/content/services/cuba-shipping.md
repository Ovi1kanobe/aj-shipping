Crev Shipping provides comprehensive shipping services to Cuba, connecting your loved ones with essential goods and personal items. We understand the unique requirements for shipping to Cuba and have established reliable networks throughout the island nation.

Our extensive experience in Cuban logistics ensures your packages reach their destination safely and efficiently, whether you're sending to major cities or remote rural areas.

### Major Cities We Serve

We provide reliable shipping services to all major Cuban cities and provinces:

1. **Havana (La Habana)** - Capital city and surrounding metropolitan area
2. **Santiago de Cuba** - Second largest city in eastern Cuba
3. **Camagüey** - Central Cuba's largest city
4. **Holguín** - Major city in eastern Cuba
5. **Santa Clara** - Central Cuba transportation hub
6. **Guantánamo** - Eastern province coverage
7. **Pinar del Río** - Western Cuba including tobacco regions
8. **Matanzas** - Including Varadero and coastal areas

### Remote Areas & Rural Coverage

Our network extends beyond major cities to serve rural communities:

- **Sierra Maestra Mountains** - Remote mountain communities
- **Isla de la Juventud** - Special municipality island coverage
- **Rural Tobacco Farms** - Vuelta Abajo region in Pinar del Río
- **Coastal Fishing Villages** - Remote coastal communities throughout the island
- **Sugar Mill Towns** - Rural agricultural communities
- **Mountain Villages** - Escambray and Sierra Maestra regions

### Access Methods

We utilize multiple shipping routes and methods to ensure reliable delivery:

1. **Air Freight** - Fast delivery to José Martí International Airport (Havana)
2. **Ocean Freight** - Cost-effective shipping via Port of Havana
3. **Regional Distribution** - Local network for nationwide coverage
4. **Rural Delivery** - Specialized service for remote areas

### What You Can Send

- Personal care items and clothing
- Non-perishable food items (restrictions apply)
- Electronics (subject to Cuban regulations)
- Household goods and appliances
- Educational materials and books
- Medical supplies (with proper documentation)
